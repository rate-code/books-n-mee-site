Chapter 1

At 35,000 feet.

No Wi-Fi, no signal, no problem. Start the timer, read for as long as the flight lets you, and tap save. Your session is stored on the phone and syncs when you land. Notes and finishing a book work the same way.

  • The timer keeps counting with the screen off.
  • Reading reminders are set on your phone, so they still fire in the air.
  • Nothing says "something went wrong" when you are offline.

Chapter 2

Add it your way.

Search the catalogue, scan the barcode, or type the book in by hand. If it is a book nobody has listed yet, share it with other readers and the next person finds it in search.

  • Ordered it online? Add the order date and the expected day. We remind you if it has not shown up.
  • Wishlist, planned, ordered, received, reading, paused, finished. One shelf, filtered by status, ownership and format.
  • Export your whole library whenever you like.

Chapter 3

Keep the line.

Highlight a line in one of five colours, write a note against the page or chapter, add tags, even photograph the page. Mark your favourites, and publish any note to Explore when you want other readers to see it.

  • Notes and highlights in one timeline, per book or across all of them.
  • Export every note when you want them elsewhere.

Chapter 4 · built by readers

Which one comes first?

Series are hard to untangle. Here, any reader can add a book to a series and put the books in order, and everyone who finds that book sees the whole list. "Book 2 of 3" appears on your book without any work from you.

  • Start a series from any book on your shelf.
  • Add the others by search, or type them in.
  • The more readers add, the better every series gets.

Chapter 5

Other readers, one tap away.

Search any reader by username, open their profile and see what they are reading and what they finished. Comment on notes and reviews to give feedback, and build your own profile with your genres and a year of reading.

  • One switch makes your account private. Only your name and photo stay visible.
  • Block anyone. They cannot open your profile or comment, and they are not told.

Chapter 6

The last page.

Finish a book and Books n Mee celebrates, then turns it into a story-sized card: the cover, how many days it took, your reading time, pages and rating. Send it to Instagram, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or anyone.

  • Rate it and write a review that stays private or goes on your profile.
  • Share from the dialog, or later from the finished book.

Chapter 7

Your year, at a glance.

A month tracker shows the days you read, stats show minutes and pages, and a year view shows how much you read in total. Set a goal in books, pages or minutes and earn badges for streaks along the way.

  • Light and dark themes, warm paper by day and forest green by night.
