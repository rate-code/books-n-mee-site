Three steps, then repeat.

  1. Add a book

    Search by title, scan the barcode or type it in. Mark it as wishlist, ordered or already on your shelf.

  2. Read with the timer

    Start the timer when you sit down. Add notes and highlights as you go. Everything saves, with or without a signal.

  3. Finish and share

    Rate it, review it, and turn the finish into a card for Instagram, WhatsApp or LinkedIn.

What it does

Read

A timer that keeps up with how you really read.

  • Reading timerRuns in the background with a sticky notification, so it keeps counting when the screen is off.
  • Works offline, even on a planeSave sessions, add notes and finish books with no internet. Books n Mee syncs when you reconnect.
  • Reading remindersA nudge at the time you choose. Set on your phone, so they work without a connection.

Remember

Keep what a book gave you.

  • NotesWrite a thought against a page or chapter, add tags and a photo of the page.
  • HighlightsSave a line in five colours and find it again by book, tag or favourite.
  • Export your notesYour words are yours. Take them out whenever you like.

Organise

Your shelf, and the books on their way to it.

  • Library with filtersFilter by status, whether you own it, and format. Wishlist, reading, paused, finished.
  • Track books you orderedNote the order date and the expected day, and get reminded when it should arrive.
  • Export your libraryOne file with every book you track.

Progress

See the habit, not just the pile.

  • Month trackerA calendar of the days you read, darker for longer sessions.
  • Stats pageMinutes, pages and sessions by day, week and month. Track how much you read across a full year.
  • GoalsSet a target in books, pages or minutes, and see how close you are.
  • AchievementsStreaks, first book, night owl, early bird and more.

Community

Reading is better with other readers.

  • Share notes publiclyPublish a note to Explore. Other readers can comment on it.
  • Search by usernameFind a reader, open their profile and see what they have read.
  • Build your profilePhoto, username, favourite genres, finished books and a year of reading.
  • Give feedbackComment on other readers' notes and reviews.
  • Private profileOne switch hides your stats, shelf and notes. Only your name and photo stay visible.
  • Block accountsBlocked readers cannot open your profile or comment. They are not told.

Share

Finish a book, show it off.

  • Finished-book cardA story-sized image with the cover, days taken, reading time, pages and your rating. Send it anywhere from the share menu.
  • Light and dark themesWarm paper by day, near-black forest green by night. Follows your phone or your choice.

Reading orders, built by readers.

Which book comes first? Series are surprisingly hard to untangle. In Books n Mee, any reader can add a book to a series and put the books in order. Everyone who finds that book sees the whole series.

  • Start a series from any book you own.
  • Add the other books by search, or type them in.
  • Other readers see "Book 2 of 7" on theirs, with no work on their side.

The more readers add, the better every series gets.
